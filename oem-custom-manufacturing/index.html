How a custom manufacturing project runs

Lead times, inspection arrangements, production quantities, export logistics and payment terms are subject to quotation and written confirmation.

  1. Submit drawings and requirements

    Drawings, specifications or a bill of materials, with quantities and target dates.

  2. Technical review

    JASSCO reviews the technical requirements and raises any questions.

  3. Feasibility discussion

    Material, tooling, manufacturing feasibility and finishing requirements are discussed.

  4. Quotation and terms

    Commercial quotation and production terms are agreed.

  5. Production, inspection and packing confirmed

    Inspection and packaging requirements are confirmed before production.

  6. Dispatch and delivery

    Dispatch and delivery arrangements, including Incoterms, are agreed.

What to send with an OEM enquiry

  • Drawings with revision numbers (PDF) and CAD files (DXF / STEP)
  • Bill of materials for assemblies
  • Material grades and any approved substitutes
  • Finish, coating and packaging specifications
  • Annual volume, batch size and call-off pattern
  • Inspection, documentation and approval requirements (e.g. first-article)
  • Delivery destination and preferred Incoterms
