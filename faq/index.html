General questions

What does JASSCO Steel manufacture?

Perforated metal sheets and coils, expanded metal mesh, perforated and expanded tubes, cable trays, custom sheet metal and laser-cut components, turned components, fabricated metal products, industrial racks and noise barriers.

Where is JASSCO's factory?

The manufacturing unit is at B-337, RIICO Industrial Area, Ghiloth, Neemrana, Rajasthan 301705, India. The corporate office is in Rajouri Garden, New Delhi.

How long has JASSCO been in business?

JASSCO has been active in metal perforation since 1990.

Do you manufacture to customer drawings?

Yes. Most work is produced against the buyer's approved drawing and revision. Laser-cut parts are programmed from DXF files.

What files can I send with a quotation request?

PDF, DXF, DWG, STEP, IGES, JPG, PNG or ZIP — up to 5 files, 10 MB each, through the request-for-quotation form. Larger packages can be arranged by email.

What materials do you work with?

Depending on the product, the catalogue lists mild steel, galvanised steel, stainless steel, aluminium, copper and brass, as applicable. Suitability is confirmed per enquiry.

What is the maximum sheet size for laser cutting?

The 2 kW fiber laser has a 1,500 × 3,000 mm bed.

How thick can you bend?

CNC hydraulic bending is listed up to 10 mm (3,000 mm width) and up to 8 mm (4,000 mm width).

Do you supply international buyers?

We welcome enquiries from international buyers. Export packing, documentation, Incoterms and logistics are agreed in each quotation.

Do you offer powder coating?

Yes, there is an in-house powder-coating facility: surface preparation, powder application, curing and final inspection.

Are you ISO certified?

Certifications will be published on our Quality page with full details once verified. If your approval process requires specific certifications, please raise this at enquiry.

What are your lead times and minimum order quantities?

Lead times and order quantities depend on the part, material and process, and are confirmed in each quotation.
