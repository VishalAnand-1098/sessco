Our story

Active in metal perforation since 1990, JASSCO has built its reputation through longstanding customer relationships and its own manufacturing facilities.

We support requirements ranging from individual fabricated metal parts to fully assembled products. Our business serves OEMs, fabricators and point-of-purchase applications, directly and indirectly supporting the supply chains of major brands.

Our capabilities include design and engineering support, CNC punching and forming, laser cutting, welding, perforating, assembly, finishing, packaging and fulfilment. We continue to invest in production quality and efficiency, with a focus on continuous improvement, consistent products and dependable delivery.
