Manufacturing process

From material to finished component

Sheet metal processing, component manufacturing and fabricated products — brought together under one manufacturing programme.

  1. 01

    Sheet metal cutting

    Laser cutting, perforation and shearing produce precision profiles and blanks.

  2. 02

    Metal forming

    Bending, rolling and forming create complex shapes and functional components.

  3. 03

    Welding

    MIG, argon (TIG), pulse, linear and seam welding to suit the component and finish.

  4. 04

    Punching & perforating

    Accurate holes and repeatable patterns for ventilation, screening and aesthetics.

  5. 05

    Expanded metal

    Slitting and stretching sheet into an open-grid structure for lightweight products.

  6. 06

    Finishing & assembly

    Powder coating, assembly, packaging and fulfilment complete the programme.

Manufacturing facility

A connected facility in Rajasthan's Neemrana industrial belt

Our manufacturing unit at B-337, RIICO Industrial Area, Ghiloth, Neemrana covers a total factory area of 8,000 square metres. Cutting, perforating, expanding, forming, machining, welding, powder coating and assembly sit under one roof, supported by 5-ton and 12-ton EOT overhead cranes for material movement.

Our corporate office is in Rajouri Garden, New Delhi.
